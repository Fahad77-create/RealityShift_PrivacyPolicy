1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information WorldFlip.io: One Shape Two Worlds ("the app", "we", "us") handles when you use our mobile game, how that information is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that store your personal data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (completed rooms, stars, coins, hints, unlocked cosmetics), your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics and accessibility options) and the state of the Daily Space Chest are saved in the app's private storage on your device. To protect the Daily Space Chest from tampering, the app creates a signature using a device identifier available to the app. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising Services

The game does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera or microphone data. However, our advertising service may automatically collect and process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID.
  • Device type, operating system version, language and app version.
  • Advertising interactions (ads shown, taps, rewards earned) and diagnostic information.

We do not use this information ourselves and we do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To save your progress and settings on your device.
  • To display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements, including rewarded ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards.
  • To measure ad performance, prevent fraud and keep advertising services working (performed by Google).
  • To confirm an internet connection and apply the Daily Space Chest rules.

5. Advertising Services

Our application uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information to provide, personalize, measure and improve advertisements.

For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Internet Connectivity

An internet connection is required to play. The app sends simple HTTPS requests to public connectivity and time servers (for example Google and Cloudflare) to check that your device is online and to read the current UTC time used by the Daily Space Chest. These requests contain no personal information from the game, but, as with any internet request, the receiving server can see your IP address. An internet connection is also needed to load and display advertisements.

7. Data Sharing

The only third party that receives information through the app is Google (AdMob). We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with anyone else. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's privacy policies and practices.

8. Your Choices

  • Advertising ID and personalization: on your Android device, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to opt out of ad personalization or to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.
  • Local data: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings to delete everything stored on your device.

9. Data Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the application. Third-party services, including Google, apply their own security measures.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected through Google AdMob is handled according to Google's data retention and deletion policies. You can also contact us at the email below with any request about your data.

11. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we request ads at the most restrictive content rating (G). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

12. International Transfers

Google may process information in countries other than your own. Its safeguards are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
